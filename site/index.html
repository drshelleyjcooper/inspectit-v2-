Why It Matters

A missed oil change, a small roof leak or an expired warranty rarely looks urgent. Left alone, each one grows into a breakdown, water damage or a bill you thought was covered. Most owners and managers aren’t careless; the records are just scattered across glove boxes, inboxes and filing cabinets. Inspectit.app puts them in one place and tells you what needs attention next.

  • Prevent. Brakes, tires and roof wear on predictable schedules. Regular inspections and planned maintenance catch problems while they are still small and cheap.

  • Prove. A documented service history makes a vehicle more attractive to buyers, and a complete home record shows the property was well cared for. The same records back up insurance and warranty claims.

  • Protect. Consistent tracking keeps people safe and helps organizations stay compliant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how Inspectit.app works.

Who can use Inspectit.app?

The application is designed for individuals, companies, organizations, property managers, fleet managers, inspectors, maintenance personnel, and others responsible for managing vehicles, properties, or projects. It also supports vehicles and properties that have been adapted for aging individuals or people with disabilities.

What is this application designed to do?

The application provides one place to manage inspections, maintenance schedules, repair tickets, warranties, documentation, and remodeling projects for vehicles and properties. It helps individuals, companies, and organizations keep important information organized and maintain an ongoing history of inspections, maintenance, repairs, and projects.

How does this application help prevent costly repairs and improve safety?

The application creates a continuous record of inspections, scheduled maintenance, repairs, warranties, and projects, helping users identify potential problems, document corrective actions, monitor overdue items, and maintain historical records. For organizations and commercial entities, it also provides a centralized way to support ongoing maintenance, safety practices, documentation, and compliance activities.

Can I manage both vehicles and properties in the same application?

Yes! Users can create individual records, or “cards,” for vehicles and properties. Each card can contain its own inspections, maintenance schedules, repair tickets, warranties, documents, costs, and historical records.

How do repair tickets help track repairs?

Repair tickets provide a detailed record of each repair, including who recommended the repair, the repair provider or vendor, status, date completed, cost, odometer reading when applicable, description, and notes. Related invoices, receipts, and other documentation can also be attached to the repair record.

Can the application generate reports and track costs?

Yes. Users can generate inspection, maintenance, and repair reports containing dates, descriptions, status, and costs. Reports can be generated for monthly, quarterly, yearly, or customized date ranges. The system can also provide an ongoing history and help users track the total amount spent on maintenance and repairs.

Can I use the application to manage remodeling or improvement projects?

Yes. Property owners and organizations can create projects for remodeling, major repairs, energy-efficiency improvements, accessibility modifications, adaptive-use projects, or other types of improvements. Projects can track goals, estimates, materials, expenses, permits, design fees, change orders, contractors, subcontractors, schedules, and project documentation from beginning to completion.

Can companies and organizations control what users are allowed to do?

Yes. Organizations can assign different user roles based on responsibilities. For example, users may be assigned as vehicle managers, property managers, project managers, inspectors, maintenance personnel, or viewers. This allows organizations to give personnel access appropriate to their responsibilities while maintaining centralized records for vehicles, properties, and projects.

What kinds of remodeling projects does it help with?

We support:

  • Home repairs and regular maintenance
  • Accessibility improvements
  • Renovation, planning and budgeting
  • Tracking service providers with full documentation
What makes Inspectit.app different from other tools?

Inspectit.app is everything in one place: inspections, maintenance, repairs, warranties and documents, and remodeling projects. It has a human-centered design and capacity for personalized inspection and maintenance forms. We focus on making the process feel manageable and empowering.
