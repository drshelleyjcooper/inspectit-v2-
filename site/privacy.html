Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how we handle your personal information. We have tried to write it in plain language.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (account details and your records) plus basic technical details like your IP address for security.
  • We use it to run Inspectit.app for you. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.
  • A few companies help us run the service (hosting, email and video). We name them below.
  • If a trial ends without a subscription, the account is paused and becomes read-only. We keep records 30 more days, warn you, then delete them.
  • You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time: info@inspectit.app.

Who we are

Inspectit.app is based at 1290 W. Gregory Blvd., Kansas City, MO 64114. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or use the Inspectit.app app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. For questions, email info@inspectit.app.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. We never store your password itself, only a scrambled (“hashed”) version of it.
  • Optional sign-up answers: what you will track (vehicles, properties or both), whether it is for you or an organization, the organization’s name, and roughly how many vehicles or properties you manage.
  • Your records: everything you or your team enter or upload, such as vehicle and property details, inspections, maintenance schedules, repair tickets, costs, warranties, contractor and vendor details, notes, photos and documents.
  • Demo requests: the name, email address and number of vehicles or properties you tell us in the Schedule a Demo form.
  • Billing details: for paid plans, the payment information you give Stripe when you subscribe. See “Who we share it with.”
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical details: your IP address and browser type when you use the service or fill in a form. We record these in security and activity logs, for example when you sign in or when records are created or changed.
  • Usage basics: things like when you last signed in and which organization an action belongs to. We use these to keep the service working and secure.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising trackers on our website, and we do not run third-party analytics on it. We do not collect or store payment card numbers ourselves. Payments for paid plans are handled by Stripe (see “Who we share it with”).

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and your organization’s workspace, and to show your records to the people you give access to.
  • To send you messages about the service, such as welcome and sign-in help, password reset links, invitations, trial reminders, account notices and replies to your questions or demo request.
  • To keep the service safe: preventing abuse and spam, limiting repeated sign-in attempts, finding and fixing problems.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.
  • To improve Inspectit.app, using general information about how the service is used.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your records for advertising.

Who we share it with

People in your organization. If you use Inspectit.app through a company or organization, its administrators decide who can see what. People you invite can see the records their role allows.

Companies that help us run the service. They only handle information to do their job for us:

  • DigitalOcean hosts the service, our database and the files you upload. Our servers are in the United States.
  • Resend delivers the emails we send you from the service.
  • Microsoft 365 runs our business mailbox, where we receive demo requests, notifications and any email you send us.
  • YouTube (Google) provides our demo video. The video only loads when you open it, and we use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. Once it loads, YouTube may collect information as described in Google’s privacy policy.
  • Your calendar app (for example Google, Apple or Outlook), if you choose to subscribe to your private calendar link. That app fetches the dates and titles in the link.
  • Stripe processes payments for paid plans. Stripe collects your payment details directly and handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive only limited billing information from Stripe, such as your plan, whether your payment succeeded, and when your subscription period ends. We do not store your full card number.

When the law requires it. We may share information to follow a legal process, to enforce our terms, or to protect people’s safety and the security of the service.

If our business changes. If we merge with or are bought by another company, your information may transfer to it. We will tell you and it must honor this policy or give you a choice.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is active, we keep your account details and your records so the service can work for you.
  • If a free trial ends and you do not subscribe, you have 7 days of continued access. Then the account is paused, which makes it read-only (you can view records but not change them), and we keep the organization’s records for 30 more days. We email a final warning about 7 days before they are deleted. After that, the records are hidden for 7 more days in case of a mistake, then permanently deleted: the database records, the files you uploaded, and the accounts of members who belonged only to that organization. The same applies if you ask us to delete an organization’s records.
  • If you ask us to close your account or delete your information, we will do so within a reasonable time, unless we must keep something to follow the law, settle a dispute or prevent fraud.
  • Backups and logs. Copies of deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 30 days, and security logs are kept for up to 30 days, before they are overwritten or deleted.
  • Demo requests are kept as long as we need them to follow up and keep business records, and we delete them if you ask.

How we protect it

We use encryption (HTTPS) to protect information as it travels between you and our servers. Passwords are stored in hashed form. Access to records is limited by the roles an organization assigns, and we limit repeated sign-in attempts. Administration tools are limited to authorized staff.

No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please choose a strong password and do not share it. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

Cookies and browser storage

Our website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s storage in small ways that help the pages work, for example to remember that you have already seen the opening animation during your visit. The Inspectit.app app uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and to keep a copy of your records on your device so they work smoothly. You can clear this in your browser settings, but you may be signed out and lose data that has not synced.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information or close your account;
  • stop sending you non-essential emails (service emails such as password resets and account notices will still be sent).

Email info@inspectit.app from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it is you. We will answer within 45 days. Depending on where you live (for example, California and some other states), you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and you may be able to complain to a regulator. We will not treat you unfairly for using your rights. If your records belong to an organization, the organization’s administrator controls them and we may direct your request to that organization.

Children

Inspectit.app is for adults and organizations. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where information is processed

Our service and our providers are based in the United States, and your information will be processed there. If you use Inspectit.app from another country, you understand that your information will be sent to the United States, where privacy laws can differ from those where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change, we will email the account’s administrators or post a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact us

Email info@inspectit.app, or write to us at Inspectit.app, 1290 W. Gregory Blvd., Kansas City, MO 64114.
